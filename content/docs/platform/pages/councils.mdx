The **Councils** area is where you assemble [councils](/docs/building-blocks/workflows/councils) — structured groups of agents that collaborate on work a single agent can't handle alone.

## Resources it contains and manages

| Resource | What it manages |
|----------|-----------------|
| Councils | Leader agent, member agents, delegation strategy (dynamic / broadcast / decompose / round-robin / vote). Councils are primarily referenced as steps inside routines rather than executed standalone |

## Building a council

When you create or edit a council you set:

- **Leader** — the agent that coordinates the group and synthesizes the result.
- **Members** — the additional agents, each with a distinct role. Keep them deliberately different; a council of near-identical agents adds cost without signal.
- **Delegation strategy** — how the leader distributes work: dynamic, broadcast, decompose, round-robin, or vote.

A council is typically used as a **single step inside a [routine](/docs/platform/pages/routines)** rather than run on its own. For when to use a council, the strategies, and member-design guidance, see [Councils](/docs/building-blocks/workflows/councils).
